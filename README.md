🍽️ Restaurant Order Analysis Dashboard

 Project Overview

This project analyzes restaurant sales and customer ordering behavior using SQL and Power BI. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights that can support decision-making related to menu performance, revenue optimization, and operational efficiency.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of restaurant performance through key business KPIs, sales trends, product analysis, and customer purchasing patterns.

---

 Business Objectives

The analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

* How much revenue is the restaurant generating?
* Which menu items are the most popular?
* Which menu items generate the highest revenue?
* Which food categories contribute the most to sales?
* What are the restaurant's peak operating hours?
* How do order volumes change over time?
* How many items do customers purchase per order?

 Key Insights

* The restaurant generated **159.22K** in revenue from **5,370 orders**.
* **Hamburger** was the most frequently ordered menu item.
* **Korean Beef Bowl** generated the highest revenue.
* **Italian cuisine** was the highest revenue-contributing category.
* Peak customer demand occurred at **12:00 PM**, indicating strong lunch-hour traffic.
* Customers purchased an average of **2.28 items per order**, suggesting opportunities for cross-selling and combo promotions.

---

 Business Recommendations

* Increase staffing during peak lunch hours.
* Promote high-revenue menu items to maximize profitability.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing categories such as Italian cuisine.
* Introduce bundled meal offers to increase average order size.
* Monitor low-performing menu items for potential optimization.
